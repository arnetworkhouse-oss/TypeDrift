About AR TypeLab

Built to measure real typing skill

AR TypeLab is a browser-based typing practice and speed testing tool developed to help users measure their typing speed, improve accuracy, and build better typing consistency.

Every test checks your input character by character, so you always know exactly what was typed correctly, what was missed, and where you are in the passage. Results are calculated using standard WPM and accuracy formulas, and your history stays saved privately in this browser.
